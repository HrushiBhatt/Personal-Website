About Me

Open to Summer 2026 Internships

I am a Computer Engineering student at Iowa State University, graduating in Spring 2027 with a concentration in Software Development and Artificial Intelligence. I enjoy building things that live at the intersection of hardware and software, from low-level firmware all the way up to full backend systems.

On the professional side, I interned as a Product Engineer at Motorola Mobility in Chicago, where I worked on data migration pipelines that served over nine million users worldwide. My project work includes a fully pipelined five-stage RISC-V processor built in structural VHDL and an autonomous radar-mapping robot running on an ARM Cortex-M4.

I am actively looking for Software Engineering internships in backend development, embedded systems, or applied AI and machine learning. My goal is to work on software that is fast, dependable, and makes a real difference for the people who use it.

Outside of engineering, I have played competitive & club Ultimate Frisbee for 10+ years and currently serve as Vice President of the Iowa State club team. I also enjoy golf, follow Formula One racing as a Red Bull fan, cheer for the Chicago Bears, and love traveling. I have visited seven countries so far with more on the list.
